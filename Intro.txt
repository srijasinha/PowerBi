Q)What are Sliecers and Filters?
Ans -Slicers are dynamic, interactive visuals on the report page that allow users to directly filter data.
Filters, on the other hand, are hidden options applied at various levels (visual, page, allowing users to filter data directlyeport) and control what data is displayed, often behind the scenes.
